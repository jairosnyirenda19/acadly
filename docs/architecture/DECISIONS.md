- Document management (raised by the product owner, 15 November 2026; to be discussed in depth before it is designed). The school keeps the full record of every learner, so documents must be a first-class capability, not only attachments. Points raised:
  - A robust document store that can hold a large number of documents per learner and per school.
  - Scanned copies of marked work: a school can scan a marked examination paper, keep the digital copy, and give the paper back to the learner. This must be an option the school can choose.
  - One document can belong to more than one record (for example a learner, an assessment and a class). The link from a document to its owners is therefore many-to-many and must respect sealed modules (a link through `Contracts/`, no cross-module relations).
  - Decided 16 November 2026: one documents table and one files table in the kernel, each with a clear owner, links to other records, versions, trash and holds (D-053 to D-055).
  - Still to decide: retention and erasure per data class; who can see a scanned script; bulk scanning and how scans are matched to learners; page previews and OCR; storage size per school.
